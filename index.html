What can MDI Icon Search do?

Search all 7,400+ Material Design Icons used by Home Assistant, by name, alias, tag or even in Dutch. Click to copy mdi:name, YAML or the SVG.

  • Search 7,400+ icons by name, alias and category, instantly
  • Dutch search words work too (lamp, deur, wasmachine, …)
  • Preview in your own colour and size, light or dark
  • Copy mdi:name, a YAML icon: line, the SVG or the path data
  • Browse by category, recently copied icons are remembered
  • Deep links: #lightbulb opens that icon directly

Frequently asked questions

Which icons can I use in Home Assistant?

Home Assistant includes the full Material Design Icons set. Any icon from this search works as icon: mdi:name in cards, entities and dashboards, without installing anything.

How do I change an entity icon?

Open the entity in Settings → Devices & services → Entities, click the settings cog and paste mdi:name in the Icon field. In YAML cards use icon: mdi:name.

Can I search in Dutch?

Yes. Common Dutch words like lamp, deur, wasmachine, verwarming and zonnepanelen are translated to the right English icon names.

Can I download an icon as SVG?

Yes. Pick an icon and use Copy SVG or Download SVG; your chosen colour is applied to the SVG.
